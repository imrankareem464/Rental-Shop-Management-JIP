RENTAL SHOP MANAGEMENT SYSTEM — ENGLISH EDITION
=================================================

OPEN THE SOFTWARE
Double-click index.html, or right-click it and choose Open with > Google Chrome.
No installation, internet, subscription or Microsoft Access is required.
Keep index.html, style.css and ALL .js files together in the same folder.

GET STARTED
1. Open Settings and enter your organization name, address and contact details.
2. Add shops, including shop number, business type, block and floor.
3. Add tenants and their contact details.
4. Create rental contracts with start/end dates and monthly rent.
5. Choose a reporting month at the top of the screen.
6. Record rent payments in Rent Collection; print the generated receipt.

MENUS
Dashboard: totals, collection chart, occupancy, expiries, arrears, payments,
notifications, expenses, block/floor summary and quick actions.
Shops: add/edit shops, track vacant and rented units, business types.
Tenants: add/edit tenant directory, contact details, ID and address.
Contracts: create agreements, track dates, deposit reference and due day.
Rent Collection: full/partial payments, Received/Pending filters, rent bills.
Receipts: print rent receipts and void incorrect payments with a reason.
Outstanding: unpaid months through the reporting month and collection actions.
Notices & Reminders: automatic drafts when opening this menu; view, edit,
save, print, and manually mark as delivered. No email or SMS is sent.
Renewal & Increase: renew contracts and schedule effective-month increases.
Expenses & Maintenance: costs, categories, vendors, properties and job status.
Utilities: electricity/water/gas/service bills, payment records and receipts.
Reports: rent, payments, outstanding, expenses and utilities; CSV and print/PDF.
Settings: organization preferences, JSON backup/restore and demo workspace.

KEEP YOUR RECORDS SAFE
Data is saved only in the current browser profile for this file location.
This is an offline, single-user app; it has no shared database or login.
Use Backup regularly. Clearing browser data may remove your records.
Moving or renaming the HTML file, or switching browsers, may make your existing
records unavailable until you restore a JSON backup.

MOVE TO ANOTHER PC
1. Download a JSON backup from the current app before transferring.
2. Copy the entire application folder and your JSON backup to the other PC.
3. Open index.html in Chrome and use Settings > Restore JSON Backup.
4. Select your backup and confirm. Restoring replaces the current workspace.
Changes on separate PCs do not synchronize automatically.

ORIGINAL URDU DATA
The old Urdu app is preserved as urdu-original.html.
When index.html stays in the original folder and browser, the English app
attempts to import the old Urdu app's saved local data on its first opening.
You can also restore an original Urdu JSON backup using Settings.
Microsoft Access .accdb and .mdb files are not supported.

ACCOUNTING RULES
- Full monthly rent applies to first and last contract months; no daily proration.
- A shop cannot have two contracts within the same billing month.
- Renewals default to the first day of the month after the old contract ends.
- Monthly collection totals use the billing month, not cash receipt date.
- Outstanding includes unpaid rental months from contract start through the
  selected reporting month. Utilities are listed separately.
- Receipts retain the tenant/shop names as they were at payment time.
- Void payments stay in the audit history and restore outstanding rent.
- Increases cannot change months for which payment records already exist.
- Deposit is a reference amount, not a record of deposit payment.
- Net rental income is collected rent less recorded operating expenses.
  Security deposits and utility collections are excluded from this number.
- Utility collections record full bill payment. Supplier costs can be entered
  separately under Expenses.

DEMO
The first dashboard offers Explore demo data. All demo records are fictional.
Loading demo data replaces the current workspace after confirmation. Back up
real records first. Use Settings > Start empty workspace to start real entry.

PRINT
Open a receipt, bill, agreement, reminder or report, then click Print / Save PDF.
In Chrome's print screen select your printer or Save as PDF.
